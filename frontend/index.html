Platform

Digital platforms for youth employment

Digital professional platforms may give some young people a more effective way to find work than traditional job search methods. CareerPath brings career exploration, role comparison, and learning guidance together in one place.

The platform directly addresses gaps in career guidance, especially for youth with limited access to schools, networks, institutions, and work experience.

Across Africa, over 72 million young people are not in employment, education, or training (NEET), with two-thirds being young women.

Backend

Structure that scales with users, jobs, and applications

The basic data model includes four core entities: Users, Job Listings, Applications, and Messages. This architecture is strong enough to power an online job portal and career platform.

Users are defined by a unique ID, username, email, password, and role. Job listings reference employers and organize relevant attributes like title, description, location, and salary.

  • User management and role assignment
  • Job creation and publishing workflows
  • Candidate application tracking
  • Messaging and engagement records
